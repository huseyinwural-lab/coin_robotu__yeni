**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Tiered Scanning:** The new core architecture. A 3-layer pipeline to manage performance.
    -   **Layer-1 (Discovery Scan):** Lightweight, high-recall scan on the full  universe.
    -   **Layer-2 (Qualification Scan):** Medium-cost analysis (e.g., trend, liquidity) on a capped set of discovery candidates.
    -   **Layer-3 (Decision Kernel):** Heavy, full strategy execution on a small, highly qualified set of candidates to produce  decisions.
-   **Bot-Driven Universe Selection:** The bot sees the entire market; its own logic, not config files, determines tradeable symbols.
-   **Performance Fallback:** An automatic switch to a Top Volume universe when system stress (latency, queue depth) is detected.
-   **Alembic-Only Migrations:** All DB schema changes are handled by Alembic, enforced by a strict CI gate.
-   **Freshness SLA & Backpressure:** Policies to prevent trading on stale data and to manage system load by adjusting scan intervals.
