**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Bot-Driven Universe Selection:** The core principle that the bot should see the entire market and use its own logic to select opportunities, rather than being limited by static configuration lists.
-   **Performance Fallback:** An automatic mechanism where the system detects high load (latency, queue depth) and switches from full market scanning to a less resource-intensive Top Volume mode.
-   **Backpressure Handling:** A set of strategies (deferring low-priority jobs, dropping stale jobs) to ensure system stability under heavy load.
-   **Freshness SLA:** A strict rule that prevents the engine from making decisions based on data that is older than a defined threshold, resulting in a .
-   **Tiered / Two-Stage Scanning:** A performance optimization where a lightweight discovery scan identifies potential candidates across the wide universe, and a more intensive decision scan runs only on those candidates.
-   **Indicator Caching:** Using Redis (with a DB fallback) to cache the results of expensive indicator calculations, keyed by symbol, timeframe, and bar time to avoid redundant computations.
-   **Alembic-Only Migrations:** The user mandated that all database schema changes MUST be handled through Alembic migrations to ensure consistency and prevent schema drift.
