**Code Architecture**


**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Master Signal Engine:** A central service that receives raw signals from multiple strategies, normalizes them into scores, applies weights, resolves conflicts, and produces a single, deterministic trade intent (LONG, SHORT, or NO_TRADE).
-   **Strategy Registry:** A canonical, database-driven list of the 12 approved trading strategies. This prevents the use of arbitrary or deprecated logic.
-   **Strategy Family Gating:** An admin-controlled layer that can enable/disable or set score thresholds for entire families of strategies (e.g., Trend, Reversal), allowing for dynamic adaptation to market conditions without code changes.
-   **Explainability Data Contract:** A rich, structured data payload sent from the backend to the frontend that details every step of a decision, including contribution scores from each strategy and the specific reason for any blocks.
